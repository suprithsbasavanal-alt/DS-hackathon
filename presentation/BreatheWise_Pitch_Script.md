# BreatheWise — 2-Minute Competitive Pitch Script

**Format:** Spoken presentation to hackathon judging panel  
**Target Duration:** Exactly 120 seconds (calibrated to 254 spoken words at 135 words per minute conversational cadence)  
**Speaker Persona:** 2nd-year CSE Data Science Student / Senior Technical Co-founder  
**Core Theme:** Truthful Data Science, Genuine Data, CPCB Regulatory Integrity, and Transparent Model Benchmarks  

---

### [00:00 – 00:25] Opening Hook, Real Problem & Innovation Statement (66 words)
> "Good morning, judges. Most air quality apps report an opaque number without scientific context, hiding diurnal spikes and leaving citizens uninformed.
> 
> Most AQI dashboards focus on reporting a number. BreatheWise focuses on understanding the data behind that number: temporal trends, retrospective weather associations, four forecasting benchmarks, and auditable calculations in one offline platform. We use multiple stations to explore spatial patterns, while explicitly disclosing more limited hourly and weather coverage."

---

### [00:25 – 00:50] Genuine Data & Regulatory Grounding (67 words)
> "We started with zero synthetic data. We validated 50,584 daily observations from CPCB across Bengaluru, coupled with 16,080 ERA5 hourly weather records and 66,947 landmark hourly observations. We strictly enforced CPCB Table 3.2 rules, applied the 16-hour sufficiency filter—preserving 46,795 compliant records—excluded CO and Ozone from daily station AQIs, and instituted an explicit 500 ceiling policy for Severe values without claiming unverified interpolation formulas."

---

### [00:50 – 01:25] Forecast Arena & Core Scientific Finding (69 words)
> "For next-day forecasting, we evaluated a 123-day held-out test split with zero future weather leakage. Here is our core finding: our evaluation showed that the simple persistence baseline outperformed both machine-learning approaches. Persistence scored an MAE of 7.42 points, while Direct Random Forest scored 7.74 and our Two-Stage Physical model scored 7.91. This demonstrates why complex models must always be tested against simple baselines before claiming predictive value."

---

### [01:25 – 01:45] The Working Offline Product (31 words)
> "BreatheWise is a live full-stack application with five screens: Overview, Explorer, Forecast Arena, Health Advisory, and Data Transparency. Backed by 60 automated tests, it operates completely offline on localhost."

---

### [01:45 – 02:00] Closing Value Proposition & Transition to Demo (21 words)
> "BreatheWise proves that honest data, regulatory compliance, and transparent machine-learning benchmarks deliver genuine Data Science value. Thank you, and we welcome your questions."
